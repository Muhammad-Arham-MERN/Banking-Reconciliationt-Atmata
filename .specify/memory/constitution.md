# Banking Reconciliation System Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Strict Instruction Following

All development instructions, specifications, and requirements MUST be followed precisely as written. No assumptions, improvisations, or interpretations beyond the explicit text are permitted without Developer confirmation.

**Rationale**: Banking reconciliation requires accuracy and precision. Ambiguity in implementation could lead to incorrect financial calculations, making strict adherence to specifications essential for reliability and correctness.

### II. Developer Stack Authority

The technology stack choices made by Developer Muhammad Arham MUST be strictly followed. No alternative frameworks, libraries, or tools may be proposed or implemented without explicit Developer approval.

**Rationale**: Developer has specific expertise and context for this local automation tool. Stack consistency ensures maintainability and alignment with the project's efficiency goals.

### III. Supervised Collaboration

Suggestions and technical recommendations are allowed but MUST be presented under Developer supervision. All proposals must be framed as options for Developer consideration, not as autonomous decisions.

**Rationale**: Maintains Developer control while leveraging AI capabilities for optimization. Ensures technical suggestions align with project vision and constraints.

### IV. Constructive Objection

Objections to proposed approaches, requirements, or implementations are allowed and encouraged when they identify genuine risks, ambiguities, or technical concerns. However, objections MUST be constructive and include alternative suggestions.

**Rationale**: Prevents technical debt and architectural mistakes while maintaining collaborative problem-solving. Banking systems require careful consideration of edge cases and error scenarios.

### V. Controlled Creativity

Creativity and innovation are permitted but MUST be strictly controlled under Developer supervision. Novel approaches must be explicitly approved before implementation, particularly for file processing logic and discrepancy detection algorithms.

**Rationale**: Banking reconciliation is a critical financial process. Creative solutions must balance innovation with reliability and accuracy requirements.

### VI. Ambiguity Resolution

When encountering vague, ambiguous, or unclear instructions, the system MUST immediately ask Developer Muhammad Arham for assistance and clarification. No assumptions should be made about requirements.

**Rationale**: Financial data processing requires absolute clarity. Ambiguity in reconciliation logic could lead to incorrect discrepancy detection and financial reporting errors.
